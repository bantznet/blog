Almost three years ago I fell down the awesome rabbit hole that is 3D printing, starting out with my 1st printer - a [Sovol SV06](https://wiki.sovol3d.com/en/SV06). I personally had no idea what I was doing, but had a very good - and very patient - friend who's been [printing since the days of glass plates and hairspray](https://www.reddit.com/r/3Dprinting/comments/ae8yog/when_using_hairspray_on_a_glass_bed_how_often_do/).

Since then I've upgraded to an [Elegoo Centauri Carbon](https://wiki.elegoo.com/Centauri-carbon), a printer that is so phenomenally better than the SV06 in every way that I can think of with my limited experience.

Though my track record of printers so far is mixed as

- the SV06 was a Prusa Prusa i3 MK3S+ clone
and
- the Centauri Carbon is aimed directly at replacing a Bambu Lab X1 _Carbon_

my preference for where I look for models and where I publish my own is [Printables.com](https://www.printables.com/@jessmarie). Follow me there if you'd like.

Alternatives:

- [Thingiverse](https://www.thingiverse.com/) - can search work? Please? _Please?_
- [Cults3D](https://cults3d.com/) - yes, the gloves have come off
- [Makerworld](https://makerworld.com/) - hard to use if you don't buy into the Bambu Labs ecosystem,[ which I probably won't ever do](https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2026/bambu-lab-abusing-open-source-social-contract/).
- [Yeggi](https://www.yeggi.com/) - aggregator of the above and more, so... good luck :-)
